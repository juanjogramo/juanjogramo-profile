**Primary category:** iOS Subject Matter Expert & technical leader for mobile platforms in financial products.

**Differentiators**

- Deep iOS/Swift tenure (~9 years) across consumer and banking apps
- Progression from IC → staff/lead → SME enablement
- Practical architecture coaching (VIPER/MVVM, coupling/cohesion, testing)
